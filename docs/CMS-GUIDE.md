# VECTIS CMS: how to use it

A plain-English guide to the content management system at `/admin`. It covers what each part does, the best order
to enter information, and the rules the system follows to keep the website accurate.

---

## 1. Signing in

- Go to **your-website-address/admin** and sign in with the administrator email and password.
- Forgot the password? Click **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page. A reset link is emailed to you.
- **Sign out** is at the top right. On a shared computer, always sign out.
- There is one administrator account. Never share the password in chat or email.

## 2. The golden rules

1. **Nothing is invented.** Every field starts empty. Enter only information VECTIS has confirmed.
2. **Everything starts as a draft.** Drafts are never shown on the website. You decide when to publish.
3. **Investments never mean ownership** unless VECTIS states it. Use the "nature" and "in VECTIS's own words"
   fields to describe each relationship exactly.
4. **Confidential figures stay confidential.** Stakes, amounts and internal notes go only in the
   *Confidential details* panel, which the website can never read.

## 3. Publishing, step by step

Every record has a **Publishing** box on the right of its edit page.

| Status | Meaning | Shown on the website? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Draft** | Work in progress | No |
| **Scheduled** (news only) | Published with a future date and time | Yes, automatically at that time |
| **Published** | Live | Yes |
| **Archived** | Retired and hidden everywhere | No |

- **Publish** makes a draft live. **Unpublish** takes it back to draft.
- **Archive** hides a record without losing it. You can restore it later from the list's *Archived* filter.
- **Delete permanently** is only offered for archived records, and it can't be undone.
- Linked records only appear together. For example, an investment shows only when it **and both companies** are
  published, and a project shows only when its business is published. The Publishing box tells you when
  something else still needs publishing.

## 4. Recommended order for entering content

1. **Settings:** site name, tagline, contact email, phone, address, social links, logo and default sharing image.
2. **Media Library:** upload approved photos, logos and documents (see section 6).
3. **Sectors:** the industries VECTIS works in (e.g. Oil and Gas, Power).
4. **Companies:**
   - First **VECTIS itself**, as the *Parent organisation*.
   - Then its businesses and subsidiaries, each "sitting under" VECTIS.
   - Then any **outside companies** that VECTIS invests in or works with (choose *Outside company*).
5. **Investments:** which VECTIS entity invested in which company, and the nature of each investment.
6. **Projects:** each one belongs to a business, with its location and a timeline.
7. **Leadership:** board, executives and management, with their roles in each company.
8. **News & Media:** articles and press releases. Tag the companies each item mentions.
9. **Website Pages:** the text of Home, About VECTIS, Corporate History, and the other core pages.

## 5. What each menu item does

**Dashboard.** Shows the main figures at a glance: totals, drafts waiting, unread enquiries, recent changes and recent activity.

**Companies.** VECTIS, its businesses and outside companies. Each company has:
- what kind of company it is, and which company it sits under;
- its sectors, with one main sector;
- a description, logo, cover image and contact details;
- **links with other companies**: strategic partner, joint venture or affiliate, each with a Show/Hide switch for the website;
- a gallery and supporting documents.

Its page also lists the company's investments, projects, news and people.

**Investments.**
- The investor must be VECTIS or one of its businesses. The company invested in can be any company.
- Each investment has a nature, a status and an optional date, which you should enter only if confirmed.
- The **Confidential details** panel holds the stake, amount and notes. It is for administrators only.

**Projects.**
- Each project has a business, an optional sector, a stage, dates, a description and a location (place, state, country, and an optional map position).
- The **Timeline** holds key stages. You can add, edit and reorder them, or put them in date order with one click.

**News & Media.**
- Each item has a kind (article, press release, announcement…), a headline, an excerpt, the full text and the companies it mentions.
- **Feature this item** gives it prominence on the website.
- **Schedule or back-date…** sets the date and time, in Nigeria time:
  - A **future** time schedules the item. It appears on the website by itself at that moment.
  - A **past** time publishes it now with that older date, which is useful for older press releases.

**Leadership.**
- Each person has a name with any titles, a position, a group (Board / Executive / Management), an order within that group, a biography and a portrait.
- **Roles in companies** lists the boards or companies each person serves.

**Website Pages.**
- The 8 core pages each have their own fields. Core pages can be unpublished but never deleted.
- Lists of companies, projects, news and people are filled in automatically from the other sections. You don't type them here.
- **New page** creates an extra page, such as a privacy policy. Extra pages appear under About in the menu.
- **Headings in Main text** become the "On this page" index beside the text, once a page has two or more of them.
  Use Heading 2 for each section of a page.
- **Governance > Accountability loop:** the stages of the loop, in order. The website draws them as a circle of
  arrows that returns to the first stage. Leave it empty to hide the drawing.
- **Home > Banner image:** a photo shown in indigo behind the headline. Use licensed photography only, and never
  caption a stock photo as a VECTIS site, asset or person.
- **A business's cluster** (on the website) comes from its **main sector** (Companies > the business > Sectors).

**Sectors.** The industries used to group companies and projects.

**Media Library.** All uploaded files, with the places each one is used (see below).

**Enquiries.** Messages from the website's Contact page:
- Unread messages show a dot.
- Opening a message marks it read.
- **Reply by email** opens your email program with the subject filled in.

**Audit Log.** A permanent record of every change: who changed what, and when. Click a record's name to open it.

**Settings.** The website-wide details used in the header, footer and contact information.

## 6. Images and documents

- **Images** (JPEG, PNG, WebP; up to 10 MB) are public once used on a published page. Add a short description
  ("alt text") for each image. It is read aloud to visitors using screen readers.
- **Documents** (up to 25 MB): **PDFs** can be public (downloadable from the website) or private
  (administrators only). **Word and Excel files** are always private, so publish a PDF version for the website.
- A file can't be deleted while it's in use. Its page shows exactly where it's used.
- Use only approved photographs. Never alter the VECTIS logo.

## 7. Your public website

- The website shows **only what you publish**. Until the **Home** page is published, visitors see a simple
  "being prepared" page with the logo.
- **Menu items appear by themselves.** For example, "News" appears once a news item is published, and "About" once
  the About VECTIS page is published.
- **Changes appear straight away** after you save, publish or unpublish. Scheduled news appears at its time
  (within about a minute).
- **Outside companies** never get their own page under "Businesses". They appear only on the investment pages,
  with the relationship described in VECTIS's own words.
- **Contact form** messages arrive under **Enquiries**. Each sender can send up to 5 messages an hour.
- **Logo:** the master logo is used exactly as supplied. When an approved transparent version is available,
  upload it and choose it in **Settings → Approved alternative logo**.

## 8. Lists: search, filters and sorting

Every list has a search box, filters and sortable column headings. The page address keeps your search and filters,
so you can bookmark a filtered list or refresh without losing your place.

## 9. If something goes wrong

- **A red message under a field** means that field needs correcting before saving. Nothing has been saved yet.
- **"Archive this record before deleting it"** means deletion is a two-step process, to prevent accidents.
- **"Can't be deleted yet"** means something still depends on the record, such as a company with investments.
  The message says what.
- **The website looks out of date** after a change: refresh the browser. If it still looks old, wait one minute.
- **Signed out unexpectedly:** sign in again; your saved work is never lost.
